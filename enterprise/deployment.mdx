Infino Enterprise is available as a **fully-managed service**: we run and scale it, and
you connect over `https://` with an API key. See the [Quickstart](/enterprise/quickstart).

For teams that need the data and compute to stay inside their own environment, it can also
be deployed into a customer's own cloud. If that is a requirement for you, reach out and
we will walk through the options.
