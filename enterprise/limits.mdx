documented first; the rest are being finalized and are marked as team TODOs.

## Mutation row limit

A single `update` or `delete` call targets **at most 100,000 rows**. If your predicate
matches more than that, the call is rejected rather than partially applied. Narrow the
predicate (for example by an id range or a time window) so each call stays within the
limit, and reissue until the full set is covered.
